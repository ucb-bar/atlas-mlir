# RTL timing operation coverage draft

This companion to the [draft timing contract](README.md) inventories compiler representation at `7b1e3dab55c2d0bd05d51c8f6ec48e55bcedeb58`. It records static source inspection only: no new build, simulation, timing measurement, or operation qualification was performed. All footprints below are current model assumptions awaiting selected-RTL validation, including their event ages, release ages, capacities, port assignments, and completion estimates. The [expanded catalog](operation-catalog.json) contains names, classes, and engines without timing constants.

## Denominators and scope

[`buildTable`](../../lib/AtlasTiming.cpp#L10) declares **127 exact mnemonic variants, 32 `OpClass` values, and seven engines** (`Scalar`, `Lsu`, `Mxu0`, `Mxu1`, `Vpu`, `Xlu`, `Dma`). [`AtlasOps.td`](../../include/Atlas/AtlasOps.td) defines **49 Atlas operations**: 25 encodable physical machine-family operations, one structural `atlas.start`, and 23 virtual operations. These counts describe three different abstractions and must not be used interchangeably.

The existing [selected decoder census](../selected-variant-census.md) records **99 decoder semantic modes** at selected RTL `0079c0541111197741a231c002e3843fa6f545b2`. Expanding its four DMA modes across eight channels adds 28 names, giving the timing catalog's 127 variants. Channel expansion does not multiply the evidence denominator or qualify every channel. The census reports typed representation and word emission for all 99 modes, bounded independent semantic/core evidence for 69 modes, and zero full software-admitted modes; those historical flags do not establish timing availability. Consult its [frozen ledger](../selected-variant-inventory.json) for exact evidence provenance and parameter domains.

All 32 timing classes have a case in [`footprintOf`](../../lib/AtlasTiming.cpp#L374), but case presence is only compiler coverage. `Access` represents register/row/VMEM-line events, `Hold` represents unit reservations, matrix read/write release fields represent whole-register restrictions, and `vpuLive` represents VPU overlap duration; see [`AtlasTiming.h`](../../include/Atlas/AtlasTiming.h). Unknown VMEM addresses become broad accesses and bank reservations. DMA events marked `atCompletion` are handled separately from fixed-age events. These abstractions require validation of both completeness and timing.

[`VirtualPlanner::lowerOperation`](../../lib/AtlasVirtualToMachine.cpp#L504) materializes physical instructions under bounded allocation/staging policy; emitted diagnostic delays are policy, not measured hardware bounds. Virtual operations do not receive independent machine footprints: the contract must cover their expanded physical instructions and the live resources those sequences carry.

The supported surrounding `arith.constant`, `arith.addi`, `arith.cmpi`, `cf.br`, `cf.cond_br`, and `func.return` are not Atlas mnemonic entries. BF16 edge copies may also require tensor operations. Function/container structure, state tokens, virtual handles and allocation metadata do not consume independent ISA words. Physical reduction, VLI, XLU transpose, unpack and remaining VPU kinds have no dedicated admitted virtual lowering in this slice.

## Control, CSR and completion obligations

[`collectAtlasWords`](../../lib/AtlasEncoding.cpp#L244) enforces one following nonredirecting instruction for every branch/jump and rejects a stream ending in a redirect. It does not by itself prove that the delay-slot instruction and successor are resource-safe. Direct branch/JAL displacements use the selected instruction-index PC convention; JALR uses a scalar word-index base plus signed offset. LLVM emission has a restricted constant-prefix [JALR target proof](../../lib/AtlasEncoding.cpp#L200), while timing insertion/scheduling [rejects AUIPC, JALR and JAL with a link destination](../../lib/AtlasStream.cpp#L215) because rewriting changes their PC-dependent values. Existing explicit delay operations are also rejected as timing-pass input.

CSR verifiers accept six internal addresses (`0xC00..0xC03`, `0xC10..0xC11`) with read-only restrictions for status addresses; see [`CSROp::verify`](../../lib/AtlasOps.cpp#L484). Scale registers use SELI/SELD. The `Csr` timing footprint models scalar operands and an order barrier, not CSR state changes, status polling, ownership handoff, or a complete completion protocol. `Instr.release` has a CSR-only `atlas.complete` hook in [`footprintOf`](../../lib/AtlasTiming.cpp#L378) and [`dependence`](../../lib/AtlasTiming.cpp#L718), but `toInstr` does not populate `release` from physical operation attributes; it defaults false. That dormant hook must not be advertised as an active physical-stream completion guarantee.

FENCE contributes order edges rather than resource drain. ECALL/EBREAK have empty direct footprints; insertion/scheduling implements its own pre-halt drain policy. [`AtlasDelayInsertion.cpp`](../../lib/AtlasDelayInsertion.cpp#L79) deliberately terminates an inserted pre-halt stall with a NOP because halt can bypass pending delay/work. The bounded [halt observation](../scalar-memory-halt-observation.md), [FENCE no-wait observation](../fence-no-wait-observation.md), and [DELAY observation](../delay-timing-observation.md) motivate obligations; they do not certify arbitrary cross-engine drain or completion. DMA transfer completion remains event-based and requires matching waits plus declared external-memory/backpressure assumptions.

## Explicit unsupported and unqualified scope

There is no unmapped named mode within the frozen 99-row selected decoder census, and no unmapped encodable Atlas machine-family definition within the 127-name compiler catalog. This does not claim the entire ISA encoding space: arbitrary words, reserved fields, unlisted CSR addresses/forms, illegal register/pair/slot combinations and other revisions need explicit rejection or separate qualification. `findOp` rejects unlisted mnemonic strings; `encodeMachineWord` rejects operations without an encoding; virtual SSA and structural operations are intentionally outside the machine catalog. Selected decoder aliases or invalid-word behavior must be sourced and classified before admission rather than inferred from a family name.

The [census](../selected-variant-census.md) and [source discrepancies](../source-discrepancies.md) retain unresolved mode/domain gaps including remaining VPU unary modes, conversion domains, seeded accumulators/readouts, CSR/control forms, reserved-field legality, cross-family timing and integrated execution. Known model/RTL differences include DMA config, CSRRCI, square/cube encoding, VMOV width, reduction layout/order and arithmetic. A future qualified timing ledger must identify accepted operation/parameter domains and independently supported event/hold rules for all 127 names, or explicitly mark excluded variants. Unknown rules remain unsupported or conservatively blocked; the current catalog and footprints alone cannot authorize scheduling or certify completion.
